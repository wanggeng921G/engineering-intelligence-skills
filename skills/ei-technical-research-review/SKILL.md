# Technical research review

State question, inclusion criteria, time horizon, and decision context. Search primary sources first and track version/date, provenance, methods, applicability, and conflicts of interest. Compare alternatives on common criteria; distinguish reported performance from independent measurement and note missing or negative evidence.

Cite exact sections/pages/tables/test methods for consequential claims. Identify what evidence cannot establish and what experiment or source would settle the choice. Use `ei-evidence-map` for a contested decision and `ei-fact-check` for high-impact claims. Do not call a bounded search exhaustive.
