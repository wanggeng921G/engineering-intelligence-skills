# Evidence map

Frame one testable decision question and compare options under the same constraints. For every material claim record supporting evidence, strongest opposing evidence, source locator, and assumptions. Expose gaps instead of turning them into votes.

Distinguish source quality from relevance to this design. Give the decision boundary: what new measurement or fact would reverse the recommendation. A simple claim check belongs in `ei-fact-check`; do not create a graph for decoration. Record the question, options, evidence for and against, unknowns, recommendation, and reversal condition when a durable artifact is requested.
