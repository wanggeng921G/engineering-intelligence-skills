# Engineering Intelligence Orchestrator

Read `references/routing-and-risk.md` when domains overlap or risk is meaningful. Determine deliverable, artifacts, constraints, risk level, and authorization. Select relevant domain and common reliability skills; do not invoke all skills by default. If a named skill is unavailable, apply its core check directly and say what was done.

Distinguish observed facts, source-backed facts, calculations, assumptions, and proposals. State the strongest relevant failure mode and a feasible verification step. Do not imply a proposal, simulation, build, bench test, or approval occurred unless it did.

Respect project-specific instructions and existing domain skills. Do not add medical or regulatory obligations solely because a task mentions STM32. Use a brief decision record only for a consequential choice.
