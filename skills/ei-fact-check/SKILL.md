# Fact check

Restate each decision-changing claim in falsifiable form. Find original datasheets, specifications, official documentation, or direct observation; check exact part, revision, conditions, units, and date. Seek credible contradiction. Repeated secondary articles are not independent evidence.

Rate claims `verified`, `disputed`, or `unverified`; cite exact locators and limits. If safety, damage, security, or irreversible change depends on an unchecked claim, withhold a definitive conclusion and specify needed test or expert review. This is a post-draft independent pass; avoid rerunning all routine checks.
